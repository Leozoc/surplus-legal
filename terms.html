By using Surplus you agree to these terms. They are short on purpose.

What Surplus is

An iOS app that helps you eat enough to gain weight: it sets a daily calorie target, tracks what you eat against it, and suggests ways to close the gap.

What Surplus is not

It is not medical advice, and it is not a clinician.

Surplus caps how fast it will help you gain — at most 500 calories a day above maintenance, and roughly 0.5% of your bodyweight per week. It refuses to set a target at all for some people, including anyone whose current or goal weight suggests they should be talking to a professional instead. These limits are not adjustable, and no future version will make them adjustable in a way that removes them.

If you have a medical condition, take medication that affects your weight or appetite, or have any history of disordered eating, talk to a doctor before using this or any tracking app.

Calorie estimates — especially from photographs — are estimates. They will be wrong sometimes. Check anything that matters.

Subscription

Surplus costs $29.99 per year, after a 3-day free trial.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID when the trial ends.
  • It renews automatically each year unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Manage or cancel it in Settings → Manage subscription, or in your Apple ID settings. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.
  • Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under their policy, not by us.

Everything in the app requires a subscription. There is no free tier, and we would rather say that plainly here than have you find out later.

Your data
