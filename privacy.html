Privacy policy — Surplus

Last updated 6 August 2026.

Surplus is a weight-gain tracker for iOS. This policy says what the app collects, where it goes, and what is never kept. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version

  • Your meals, weight, and profile live on your device. They are not uploaded.
  • We never ask for your name, email, or any account. There is no sign-up.
  • Meal photos you scan are sent to an AI provider to be turned into numbers, and stored privately in your own folder.
  • Photos of your kitchen are deleted within seconds of being read, always.
  • Analytics records that things happened, never anything about your body.

What stays on your device

Your profile, every meal you log, every weigh-in, your targets, your progress, and your kitchen inventory are stored locally on your phone.

The consequence is worth stating plainly: if you lose or wipe your device, that data is gone. There is no account to restore it from, because there is no account. That is a deliberate trade — no sign-up screen, no email, nothing to breach — and you should know about it before it matters.

Your identity

Surplus signs you in anonymously. Our servers issue a random identifier for your install. We do not receive, request, or store your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, or Apple ID.

Nobody at Surplus can look at a row of data and know who you are.

Meal photos

When you scan a meal:

  1. The photo is resized on your device and uploaded to private storage that only your install can read.
  2. It is sent to an AI provider (currently Google) which returns estimated calories and macros.
  3. The photo stays in your private storage so it can appear alongside that meal in your own diary.

You can erase everything, including photos, from Settings → Erase everything.

We use a paid AI tier specifically because paid tiers do not use your content to train models. Free tiers of these services generally do. The difference costs us about a dollar per user per year and we consider it non-negotiable for photographs of your food.

Photos of your kitchen — the one that matters most

If you photograph a shelf, fridge, cupboard, or receipt to fill in your kitchen inventory:

That photo is deleted from our servers before the request finishes. Every time, including when the scan fails.

It is not kept for a day, or until a cleanup job runs, or under a retention policy someone could later extend. It is read, the food is listed, and it is deleted in the same request.

This is the strictest rule in the product, and it exists because a fridge is not just food. It can contain medication, alcohol, someone else's things, post stuck to the door, and sometimes another person. None of that has any business on a server, and its usefulness to us ends the moment the food is recognised.

The AI provider is also instructed to ignore and never describe people, medication, paperwork, or anything that is not food.

Menus

If you photograph a restaurant menu, the extracted text is stored and shared with other Surplus users, so the next person who eats there does not have to scan it again. The source photograph is discarded.

A menu is a public document. It contains no information about you, and nothing linking it to you is shown to anyone else.

Analytics

We use PostHog to understand whether the app works: how many people finish setting up, where they stop, whether the scanner gets used.

Every event we send is written down in advance in the app's source code, and anything not on that list is dropped before it is sent. Nothing about your body is on that list — not your weight, height, BMI, goal weight, age, or sex. Not the names of foods you log. Not photographs.

We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share data with data brokers.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, with RevenueCat managing what your subscription entitles you to. We never see your card details. Apple does not give them to us.

Your choices

  • Erase everything — Settings → Erase everything removes your local data and your stored photos.
  • Don't scan — the camera is optional. Search and manual logging work without it, and the app works fully offline.
  • Ask us — you can ask what we hold against your anonymous ID, and ask us to delete it.

Age

Surplus is not intended for under-16s and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Not medical advice

Surplus is a tracking tool, not a clinician. It caps how fast it will help you gain weight, and declines to set a target for some people on purpose. If you have a medical condition or an eating disorder, please talk to a doctor rather than an app.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will say so in the app rather than quietly updating the date.
